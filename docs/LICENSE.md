# The images in this folder

The dental X-rays shown in `demo.gif`, `overview.png`, `import.png` and `export.png` come from
**Dental caries in bitewing radiographs** by Jan Kybic, Antonín Tichý and Lukáš Kunt
(Mendeley Data, V1, 2023, [doi:10.17632/4fbdxs7s7w.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/4fbdxs7s7w.1)),
licensed under [CC BY-NC 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/).

Changes: they are shown inside Box Annotator, with boxes from the dataset's expert annotations.
The class names on the boxes (caries depths) are illustrative, not a diagnosis.
